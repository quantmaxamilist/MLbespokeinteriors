Our story

A family firm built on craftsmanship

Exceptional design, quality materials and personalised service come together to transform homes.

Founded on almost 40 years of manufacturing heritage, ML Bespoke Interiors brings together Italian elegance, German precision and British craftsmanship. With our own manufacturing facilities and a showroom in Gerrards Cross, we design, make and install every project ourselves — so you deal with one team, and one point of contact, from first sketch to final fit.
