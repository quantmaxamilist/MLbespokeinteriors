Bespoke Media Walls, Designed & Installed

A media wall is more than somewhere to place a television. It becomes the focal point of your living space — bringing together entertainment, storage, lighting and interior design in one seamless feature.

Whether you want a contemporary floating design, a feature fireplace, discreet hidden storage or a statement entertainment centre, we design, supply and install it to fit your room exactly.

Every media wall is fully bespoke, built in our own workshops with concealed cabling, integrated lighting and finishes tailored to your interior.
