Who we are

Exceptional design, beautifully made

Exceptional design, quality materials and personalised service come together to transform homes.

ML Bespoke Interiors was built on almost 40 years of manufacturing expertise. After a decade crafting luxury interiors from our Wembley showroom, we opened our home in Gerrards Cross, Buckinghamshire in 2023 — bringing our design studio, brand partnerships and craftsmanship together under one roof.

We combine Italian elegance, German precision and British craftsmanship, with our own manufacturing facilities in the UK, Germany and Italy. From luxury kitchens and fitted wardrobes to dressing rooms, media walls and home offices, everything is designed, made and installed by our own team — with one point of contact throughout your project.
