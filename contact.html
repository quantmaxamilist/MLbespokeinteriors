Good to know

Frequently asked

Where do you work?
Our showroom is in Gerrards Cross and we design and install throughout Buckinghamshire — Beaconsfield, Amersham, Chalfont St Peter, Denham and surrounding areas — as well as central and west London.
Do you make everything yourselves?
Yes. Building on almost 40 years of manufacturing heritage through Multilines Commercial, we design, manufacture and install with our own team and our own workshops, alongside our European brand partners.
Which brands do you work with?
We partner with leading European names including Caccaro, Efforma, Stosa and Raumplus, bringing together Italian elegance, German precision and British craftsmanship.
What does a project cost?
Every project is bespoke, but as a guide fitted wardrobes start from £3,500, media walls from £4,000, home offices from £5,000 and walk-in dressing rooms from £8,000. We'll give you a clear quote after your design consultation.
What happens at a design consultation?
We start by understanding how you want to use the space, then create a 3D design so you can see your room before anything is made. Consultations are free and there's no obligation.
Do you manage the whole installation?
Yes — from first design to final fit, you have one point of contact throughout, with our own craftsmen, installers and electrical and plumbing support handling every detail.
